Menus, keyboard shortcuts, links and search

The menu bar, the shortcut keys, links from notifications and the search box, drawn as they look once every fix in this part of the review is in. Each drawing sits beside today’s app at the largest and smallest window; click a drawing to try it.

TL;DR

Problem
Menus miss most pages and commands, Backlog skips ⌘4, and search shows two highlights and live-looking terminals that aren’t running.
Fix
Every command lives in the menu bar with its key, pages take ⌘1–⌘7 in sidebar order, and search has one highlight.
What users notice
⌘K finds tasks, goals, ideas, backlog and terminals, and a notification link opens on top of any dialog.
How we know it worked
Every key in the table does what its menu item says, at the smallest and largest window.

Problem

  • The menu bar has three menus. View lists five of the six pages, Ideas has no key, and Backlog sits on ⌘5 with nothing on ⌘4.
  • There’s no Edit menu, so Copy Reference and Undo can’t be found, and the debugging export sits in File.
  • Search highlights the chosen row and the row under the pointer at once, so two rows look picked.
  • Search lists terminals as “idle under a minute” while Midna isn’t running, and has no state for a slow or unreachable board.
  • A link from a notification opens its task behind an open dialog.
  • Asking Midna to show a terminal while it’s not running says “Showing it in Midna”, and nothing happens.
  • With releases off on every project, the Releases page has no way in and nothing explains why.

Solution

  • One macOS menu bar holds every command, greyed when it can’t run, with its key beside it.
  • One key table: pages on ⌘1–⌘7 in the order the sidebar lists them, and standard Mac keys left alone.
  • Search shows one highlight that follows the pointer and the arrow keys, five rows a group, and a plain line for each failure.
  • A link closes an open dialog first, keeping what was typed, then opens its task in the side panel.
  • When something can’t happen, the toast says so and stays until it’s closed.

Menu bar: every page and command, with its key

The app menu gains About, updates, hooks and the daemon restart. Edit is new, so Copy, Copy Reference and Undo have a home. View lists all seven pages. Task and Idea hold the one-click signals for the open task or idea and are greyed when none is open. The export moves to Help.

Search: one highlight, best match first

  • One highlight. It follows the pointer and the arrow keys; there’s no separate hover look.
  • Each row shows its reference, the title with the typed text marked, its status in words, and its goal. Below 640 points of width the goal drops.
  • Five rows a group, then “Show all”. The list scrolls in one place and draws only the rows in view.
  • A match found only in the description or details says so under the title.
  • Terminal rows say once that Midna isn’t running, and open the task, not the terminal.
  • ↑ ↓ move, ↩ opens, ⌘↩ opens the pull request, Esc closes.
