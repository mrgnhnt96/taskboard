    Click to drop a pin · drag to mark an area · Esc to stop

    Shortcuts

    Pan
    Scroll (Shift + scroll goes sideways), Space + drag, or drag with the middle button
    Zoom
    Ctrl/⌘ + scroll, or pinch, over the canvas
    Zoom step
    Ctrl/⌘ = and −
    100%
    Ctrl/⌘ 0
    Fit
    Ctrl/⌘ Shift 1
    Grips
    → ↑ +1px, ← ↓ −1px (Shift 10px), PgUp PgDn ±100px, Home End to the limits
    Drag, pan
    Esc cancels
    W, H
    Enter applies, Esc reverts
    Button groups
    ← → move between buttons (zoom, Light and Dark, text size)
    Screen
    Esc moves focus out of the screen, so C, ? and the zoom keys work. If the screen used the Esc (to close a menu or dialog, or clear a field), press it again
    Comment
    C turns it on or off. Click drops a pin, drag marks an area, Esc stops
    Comment box
    Ctrl/⌘ Enter saves, Esc cancels
